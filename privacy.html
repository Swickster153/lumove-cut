Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Lumove Cut is local-first: your media, projects, recordings, and exports stay on your computer. The app has no analytics or tracking.

What leaves your computer

  • License activation (Supporter only): when you activate, deactivate, or the app re-checks a license, your license key and your computer's name are sent to Lemon Squeezy to verify the license.
  • Optional downloads: downloading AI components or content packages fetches files from their hosting providers, which may log standard request data such as your IP address.
  • Bug reports: only what you choose to submit. Report a Bug opens a GitHub issue in your browser; log files are attached only if you attach them.

Local logs

The app writes a diagnostic log (lumove.log) on your computer to help troubleshoot problems. It is never uploaded automatically.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. We receive your name, email, and order details to deliver and support your license; we never see your full payment details. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy for how they process payment data.

Contact

Questions or data requests: lumovecut.support@gmail.com.
