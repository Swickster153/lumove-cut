Refund Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

If the Alpha Supporter license isn't right for you, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase — no questions asked.

How to request a refund

Reply to your purchase receipt email, or contact us at lumovecut.support@gmail.com with your order number. Purchases are processed by our reseller, Lemon Squeezy, and refunds are returned to your original payment method.

After a refund

A refunded license is deactivated. Lumove Cut keeps working as the free version, with exports up to 720p. Your projects are not affected.
